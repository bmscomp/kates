kates-operator reconciles KatesTest and KafkaCompliance in: {{ include "kates-operator.namespaces" . }}

Each of those namespaces needs a Secret named kates-connection (keys url, apiKey, optional caBundle):

  kubectl create secret generic kates-connection -n <namespace> \
    --from-literal=url=http://kates.kates.svc:8080 --from-literal=apiKey=<a key with read, test:run>

Then:

  kubectl get katestests,kafkacompliances -A
